# AI writing markers in English

Same families as the French catalogue. No single marker proves AI authorship; the pile-up does. Fix them all anyway, since each one makes the text weaker.

## 1. Statistical (rhythm and predictability)

- **Low perplexity**: every word is the most expected one. Fix with a precise verb, a concrete image, the author's own jargon.
- **Low burstiness**: sentences all 15 to 25 words with the same shape. Mix very short and long; allow fragments.
- **Uniform paragraphs**: every block 3 to 4 sentences. Let one be a single line.

## 2. Structure and syntax

- **Generic openers**: "In today's fast-paced world", "In the ever-evolving landscape of", "In the digital age", "Have you ever wondered...?", "Let's dive in". Cut; start with the point.
- **False contrasts**: "It's not X, it's Y", "This isn't just about X, it's about Y", "Not only... but also". State Y directly.
- **Rule of three**: "faster, smarter, and more efficient" every time. Use the real number of items.
- **Mechanical transitions**: Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, In addition, Firstly, Lastly, In conclusion, Ultimately, That said. Cut most of them.
- **Templated lists**: equal-length bullets, each "**Keyword**: explanation", often 3/5/7 items.
- **Signposting**: "In this article, we'll explore", "Here's the thing", "Here's why that matters", "Let's break it down".
- **Summary conclusion** plus generic engagement question ("What do you think? Let me know in the comments!").
- **Rhetorical question + answer** used repeatedly: "The result? A 30% increase."

## 3. Vocabulary

- **Overused words**: delve, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, navigate, embark, journey, unlock, unleash, harness, leverage, elevate, foster, empower, seamless, robust, pivotal, crucial, vital, paramount, transformative, game-changer, cutting-edge, groundbreaking, intricate, nuanced, multifaceted, meticulous, vibrant, bustling, ever-evolving, holistic, synergy.
- **Grandiose phrasing**: "plays a pivotal role", "serves as a testament to", "a beacon of", "stands as", "paving the way", "at the forefront of", "shaping the future", "a world of possibilities", "the power of".
- **Redundant pairs**: "clear and concise", "complex and nuanced", "vital and essential".
- **Hedges and filler**: "It's worth noting that", "It's important to remember", "Generally speaking", "In essence", "At its core", "At the end of the day".
- **Adverb stacks**: significantly, notably, truly, incredibly, seamlessly.

## 4. Typography and visuals

- **Em dash (—)** mid-sentence, especially several per paragraph. Replace with comma, colon, parentheses or a full stop.
- **Emoji bullets**: 🚀 💡 🎯 ✅ 👉 🔥 at the start of each line.
- **Markdown residue**: `**`, `###`, `---`.
- **Tracking params**: `?utm_source=chatgpt.com`.
- **Title Case Headings** where sentence case is the norm for the medium.
- **Curly vs straight quotes** mixed inconsistently.

## 5. Tone and substance

- **Mushy balance**: every claim followed by "However, it's important to consider...". Let the author commit.
- **Sanitized perfection**: no contractions, no "I", no idiom. Use contractions in anything conversational.
- **Stereotyped examples** in fictional scenarios.
- **Confident unsourced facts**: flag doubtful stats or quotes in the recap; never embellish them.
